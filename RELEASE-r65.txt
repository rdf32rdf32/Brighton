ALBION FAN HUB r65
4 August 2026

Focused mobile penalty refinement built from r64.

Changes
- Takers normally begin with their boots outside the visible 18-yard line.
- Run-up style is chosen before ball placement and retained through the kick.
- Angled, straight, curved and stuttered approaches now have distinct starting positions.
- Longer four-to-six-stride mobile run-ups with depth scaling.
- Standing-foot/contact calculation accepts the greater run-up distance.
- Instruction pill hides during the run-up so the player’s boots remain visible.
- Lighter ball shadow and smoother, less excessive mobile rotation.
- Worn penalty spot no longer resembles a second ball after contact.
- Straight run-ups use a small natural offset so the ball remains visually separate from the player.
- Slimmer goal frame and quieter side/rear netting.
- Duplicate Sound action removed from the penalty overflow menu.
- Game tabs and non-essential controls collapse during active mobile play, then return afterwards.
- Tour controls, separate Quiz/Penalties tabs, squad, fixtures, quiz and scoring balance retained.
