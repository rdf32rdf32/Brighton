# Albion Fan Hub Release 11

## Penalty shoot-out refinements

- Reduced difficulty choices to Normal and Hard.
- Anchored the goalkeeper's boots to the rendered goal line on desktop, mobile and full-screen layouts.
- Calibrated full-stretch dives from the actual goal dimensions so gloves can reach the posts and crossbar.
- Opened the saving window shortly before ball contact, with a larger and more forgiving goal-wide touch area.
- Added five stable save families: high left, low left, centre, high right and low right.
- Added distinct catches, blocks, parries and fingertip saves with follow-on ball deflections.
- Added brief goalkeeper routines: crossbar touch, pointing, glove clap and ready bounce.
- Added an active referee who checks the ball and line, whistles the kick and signals the outcome.
- Added lightweight crowd tension, hush, Brighton and Palace reactions, flags, advertising boards and an in-stand score.
- Improved Palace run-up cues, centre saves, mobile touch handling and shoot-out pacing.
- Kept one-click Albion shooting, Ready for Palace, optional Panenka, early finish, sudden death and saved records.
