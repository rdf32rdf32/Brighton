# Albion Fan Hub Release 14

## One-level penalty shoot-out rebuild

- Replaced the difficulty selector with one balanced, forgiving match mode.
- Kept Albion shooting to one action: move inside the goal and click or tap.
- Added continuous goalkeeper control: mouse movement, flicks, taps and touch swipes all work.
- Strongly rewards choosing the correct side, while preserving occasional exceptional finishes.
- Made Albion’s well-placed shots more likely to score and reduced unexplained shot deviation.
- Rebalanced Palace penalties towards realistic low, middle and central placements, with occasional misses.

## Players, referee and animation

- Reduced and re-proportioned the goalkeeper, anchored his boots to the rendered goal line and kept number 1 on his back only.
- Added longer full-stretch dives with staggered push-off, hip, torso, arm, hand, leg and landing movement.
- Enlarged and re-proportioned the striker, with smoother run-ups, planted-foot contact and follow-through.
- Added a more natural ball-placement sequence for the opening and sudden-death kicks.
- Redrew the referee at a smaller scale in a genuine side-on/three-quarter pose with checks, whistle and result signals.
- Replaced abrupt goal, miss and save poses with restrained player and goalkeeper reactions.

## Goal, ball, crowd and sound

- Rebuilt the goal as a smoother three-dimensional SVG with visible roof, rear and side netting.
- Added local net deformation for high, low, central and side-net goals.
- Improved the ball’s panels, seams, rotation, shadow, straight flight and post/crossbar behaviour.
- Added layered procedural whistle, kick, glove, net, woodwork, crowd and victory sounds.
- Improved crowd hush, wave reactions and decisive-kick celebrations without distracting during control input.
- Replaced large result cards with compact broadcast-style messages.

## Penalty Story

- Added separate post-match goal maps for Albion and Palace.
- Every kick is numbered and classified as goal, saved, woodwork or miss.
- Palace maps also show Verbruggen’s dive direction.
- Added conversion rate, save rate, clean catches, average reaction and the decisive moment.

## Whole-site polish and checks

- Improved scrolling, focus visibility, touch behaviour, mobile navigation and reduced-motion handling.
- Kept floating controls and the one-time storage notice away from the playable goal and final report.
- Updated release metadata and offline cache references consistently to Release 14.
