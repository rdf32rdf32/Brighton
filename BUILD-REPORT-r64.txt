WHY THE MOBILE DEPTH FAILED IN r63
1. shootout-r63.css enlarged the phone goal to 68–72% of stage width and 37.5–38% of stage height, while moving it to 11.5% from the top. This made the goal appear much nearer than the penalty spot.
2. The same stylesheet set both left:50%!important and top:49%!important on the taker. The penalty JavaScript tried to move the taker behind the ball using inline left/top values, but stylesheet !important declarations overrode those values.
3. Stage, player, ball and goal dimensions were changed separately rather than as one camera system.

R64 CORRECTIONS
- Mobile goal: 60–62% width, 25.5% height and 18.5% top position.
- Phone stage: approximately 410–495 px depending on viewport width.
- Taker left/top are controlled by JavaScript again.
- Player boot position is calculated from ball position and rendered player height.
- Left-footed and right-footed takers begin slightly to the appropriate side of the ball.
- Goal frame and net depth are slimmer and less visually dominant.
- Ball has clearer panels, seams, highlight and a tighter ground shadow.
- Desktop camera remains bounded and unchanged in scale.
- Dedicated desktop Tour control restored.
- Mobile Tour control restored near the top of the menu.
- Separate Quiz and Penalties tabs added to the hero and games area.
