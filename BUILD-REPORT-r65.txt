ALBION FAN HUB r65 — BUILD AND AUDIT REPORT
4 August 2026

SOURCE
Built from the complete Albion Fan Hub r64 package.

PURPOSE OF THIS RELEASE
R64 restored the mobile camera but left the taker only a short distance behind the ball. R65 makes the player normally begin the run-up outside the visible 18-yard line, while preserving the corrected goal size, ball position, Tour control and separate Quiz/Penalties tabs.

R65 PENALTY CHANGES
1. The front line of the penalty area is tied to the pitch artwork at y=570/650 of the rendered stage.
2. Mobile starting positions are anchored by the player’s boots rather than the top of the SVG.
3. The run-up profile is selected before ball placement and retained for the kick. The player therefore retreats visibly to the correct position instead of jumping when the run begins.
4. Typical starting boot positions:
   - angled: about 92.2% of stage depth;
   - curved/reverse: about 91.7%;
   - straight: about 90.7%;
   - stuttered: about 88.9%;
   - quick styles are shortened slightly but remain beyond the penalty-area line.
5. Angled, straight, curved and stuttered approaches now have distinct lateral starting positions. Straight run-ups retain a slight natural offset so the stationary ball does not cover the player’s face.
6. Mobile run-ups last longer and use four to six visible movement phases, progressive player scaling and a larger permitted contact movement range.
7. The lower instruction pill disappears during the run-up so it cannot cover the player’s boots.
8. The standing-foot/contact calculation now allows for the greater starting distance while keeping the body behind the ball until the final stride.
9. Mobile ball rotation is smoother and less excessive; the ground shadow is lighter.
10. The white worn-spot treatment that resembled a second football after contact has been replaced by a subdued patch of worn turf.
11. Goalposts and crossbar are slightly slimmer; side, roof and rear netting are quieter without enlarging the goal.
12. The duplicate Sound action has been removed from the penalty overflow menu. The main penalty sound switch remains.
13. Game tabs and non-essential controls collapse during active mobile play, including phone landscape, and return when the shoot-out ends.
