ALBION FAN HUB RELEASE 11

1. Open the Brighton GitHub repository.
2. Replace the existing repository files with every file from this ZIP.
3. Keep all files at the repository root. Do not upload the containing folder.
4. Commit the changes and wait for GitHub Pages to finish deploying.
5. Open https://rdf32rdf32.github.io/Brighton/ and press Ctrl+F5 once.

The penalty shoot-out works as follows:
- Brighton kick: wait for the referee's whistle, move inside the goal and click once to shoot.
- Palace kick: press Ready for Palace, read the run-up and click or tap the goal as the taker plants his standing foot or immediately after contact.

Difficulty has two choices: Normal and Hard.
There is no power bar and no Shoot button.
